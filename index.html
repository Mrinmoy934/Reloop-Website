10 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you send
material.

01 What condition does material need to arrive in? −

Streams are assessed on intake. Contamination, mixed fractions and packaging residue are normal — the sorting stage exists for exactly that. Send a description and we'll tell you what route it can take.

02 Do you supply material, or only take it? +

Both. Recovered streams leave as graded secondary material with a specification and an origin record, supplied directly into manufacturing operations.

03 How is traceability recorded? +

Each batch carries a digital record containing its source chain, batch chemistry, processing route and final quality specification code.

04 What volumes do you work with? +

Enquiries are matched to the stream and the processing route rather than a fixed rigid minimum. We process from pilot lots (5 MT) to continuous industrial contracts.

05 Which certifications apply? +

RELOOP facilities hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and GRS (Global Recycled Standard) chain-of-custody certifications across our material processing lines.
